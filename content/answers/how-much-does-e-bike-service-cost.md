E-bike service pricing at Haymarket Bicycles:

- **E-bike Service Level 1 ($149)** — Basic adjustment of all systems and software. Inspection of all systems and components.
- **E-bike Tire and/or Tube Repair ($35)** — Inspect and replace a tire or tube on an e-bike.
- **E-Bike Assembly ($385)** — Professional assembly of new e-bikes from the box, including safety checks and adjustments.

Call (703) 754-1911 to schedule e-bike service.
