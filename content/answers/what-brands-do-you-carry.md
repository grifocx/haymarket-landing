Haymarket Bicycles carries three brands:

- **Trek** — road, mountain, and city bikes
- **Aventon** — electric bikes
- **Time** — gravel bikes

The shop is an independent, locally owned bicycle shop — not part of a chain or franchise. It opened in 2007.
