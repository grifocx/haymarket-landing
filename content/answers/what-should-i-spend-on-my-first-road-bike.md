For a first road bike, the sweet spot for most riders is **roughly $900 to $1,800**. That buys you a reliable aluminum frame, disc brakes, and dependable components — everything a new road rider actually needs.

A few honest guidelines:

- **Under about $900** — you are looking at hybrids or entry fitness bikes (like the Trek FX), which are great bikes — just not road bikes with drop bars.
- **$900–$1,800** — the sweet spot. Entry-level road bikes here shift well, stop well, and last. Fit and maintenance matter more than price at this level.
- **$2,000–$4,000** — better wheels, electronic shifting options, carbon parts. Worth it once you know you are hooked.
- **$5,000+** — flagship carbon race bikes. Wonderful, but no beginner needs this to enjoy riding.

One rule that never fails: **fit beats price**. A $1,200 bike that fits you perfectly is faster and more comfortable than a $3,000 bike that does not.
