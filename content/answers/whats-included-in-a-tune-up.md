Tune-ups at Haymarket Bicycles come in three levels, and each level builds on the one before it:

- **Level 1 Tune-Up ($99)** — Complete safety check, gear adjustment, brake adjustment, tire inspection, and basic cleaning.
- **Level 2 Tune-Up ($159)** — Comprehensive service including full drivetrain cleaning, brake bleeding, wheel truing, and complete bike inspection.
- **Level 3 Tune-Up ($349)** — Level 1+2 plus deep cleaning and lubrication of chain, cassette, and derailleurs for optimal shifting performance.

If you are not sure which level your bike needs, call (703) 754-1911 or stop by the shop and we will help you choose.
