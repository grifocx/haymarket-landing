A professional bike fit is a one-on-one session where we adjust your bike — and sometimes your position on it — until everything works together: saddle height and tilt, reach, handlebar position, cleat placement, and more.

At Haymarket Bicycles, the **Custom Bike Fitting is $250** and uses motion capture technology to measure what your body is actually doing on the bike, not just what it looks like from the side. That lets us make precise adjustments for:

- **Comfort** — no more numb hands, hot spots on the saddle, or aching neck.
- **Efficiency** — power goes into the pedals instead of into fighting your position.
- **Injury prevention** — knee and hip strain usually traces back to a position that is slightly off.

Plan on a couple of hours, and bring your bike, your cycling shorts, and your shoes if you use clipless pedals. Call (703) 754-1911 to schedule a fit.
