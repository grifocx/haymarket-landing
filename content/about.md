# About Haymarket Bicycles

## Ownership

Haymarket Bicycles is an independent, locally owned bicycle shop in Haymarket, Virginia. It is not part of a chain or franchise.

## Opening

The shop opened in 2007 and has served Northern Virginia's cycling community for 19 years (as of 2026).

## Brands we carry

- Trek (road, mountain, city)
- Cannondale (road, mountain, e-bike models)
- Orbea (custom MyO ordering — paint and component spec)
- Aventon (electric)
- Time (gravel)

## Services we offer

- Tune-ups (Levels 1, 2, and 3)
- Drivetrain cleaning and ultrasonic bath
- Flat tire and tubeless tire service
- Suspension service
- Bike and e-bike assembly
- E-bike service
- Custom bike fitting

## Towns we serve

- Haymarket
- Gainesville
- Northern Virginia area communities
