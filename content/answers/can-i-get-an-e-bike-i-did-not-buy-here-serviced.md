Yes — you do not have to have bought your e-bike from us to bring it in for service. We work on e-bikes from major bicycle manufacturers with standard bicycle components.

To be serviceable, your e-bike needs:

- A **standard removable seat post**
- A **functional bicycle drivetrain** (regular pedals, chain, and gears)

We are unable to service scooters, electric motorcycles, throttle-only "mopeds," or models with integrated foot-pegs instead of pedals — those are a different category of vehicle with different parts and safety requirements.

If you are not sure whether your e-bike qualifies, call us at (703) 754-1911 with the make and model and we will tell you straight.
