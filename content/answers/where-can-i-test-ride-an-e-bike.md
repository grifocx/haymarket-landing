The best place to test ride an e-bike near Haymarket is our own parking lot and the roads around it — right here at 4414 Costello Way in Haymarket, just a short drive from Gainesville and South Riding.

How it works:

1. **Call ahead at (703) 754-1911.** We carry Aventon electric bikes, and model availability changes — a quick call makes sure the bike you want is on the floor and charged.
2. **Get sized.** We will set the seat and controls for you so the ride feels like it would if you bought it.
3. **Ride it.** Take a spin and get a feel for the assist, the brakes, and the handling. Bring your helmet if you have one; if not, ask and we will sort you out.

A test ride is the single best way to feel the difference between pedal assist levels and between frame styles. Stop by Tuesday through Saturday 11 AM to 6 PM, or Sunday 12 PM to 5 PM.
