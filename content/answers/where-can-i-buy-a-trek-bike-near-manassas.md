Haymarket Bicycles is a short drive from Manassas and carries Trek road, mountain, and city bikes — from the FX fitness line to the Domane endurance road line and the Fuel EX full-suspension mountain line.

Why buy your Trek from us instead of ordering online:

- **Expert help choosing** — we will match the model and size to how and where you actually ride.
- **A proper fit** — a bike that fits makes more difference than a bike that costs more.
- **Service after the sale** — tune-ups, adjustments, and repairs from certified mechanics, right down the road.
- **Warranty support** — if something goes wrong under Trek's warranty, we handle the process for you.

If we do not have the exact model and size you want on the floor, we can special-order it. Stop by 4414 Costello Way in Haymarket or call (703) 754-1911 to talk through what you are looking for.
