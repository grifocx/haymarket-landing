We are lucky here — some of the best riding in Northern Virginia starts within a few minutes' roll of the shop. These are the directions local riders go:

- **West toward the Bull Run Mountains (10–40+ miles)** — Quiet, rolling rural roads with mountain views. Paved with occasional rough patches; a gravel bike or wide-tire road bike is ideal. This is classic Fauquier County riding.
- **The W&OD Trail (as long as you want)** — The paved Washington & Old Dominion rail trail runs 45 miles from Purcellville to Arlington. Hop on near Gainesville and ride as far as you like — flat, car-free, and perfect for an easy spin or a long endurance day.
- **Gravel stretches around Haymarket (10–25 miles)** — A mix of pavement and unpaved roads to the west and south. Bring a gravel bike or 32mm+ tires and expect a few farm-road surprises.
- **Short neighborhood spins (3–10 miles)** — The streets around Haymarket and Gainesville are calm enough for after-dinner rides and warming up kids on new bikes.

A couple of local notes: drivers here are generally bike-aware but give rural roads respect — ride visible, take the lane when it is narrow, and watch for gravel at corners after dry spells.
