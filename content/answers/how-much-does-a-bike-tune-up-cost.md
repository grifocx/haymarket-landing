Tune-up pricing at Haymarket Bicycles:

- **Level 1 Tune-Up ($99)** — Complete safety check, gear adjustment, brake adjustment, tire inspection, and basic cleaning.
- **Level 2 Tune-Up ($159)** — Full drivetrain cleaning, brake bleeding, wheel truing, and a complete bike inspection on top of everything in Level 1.
- **Level 3 Tune-Up ($349)** — Level 1 and Level 2 plus deep cleaning and lubrication of the chain, cassette, and derailleurs for optimal shifting. Huge value.
- **Drivetrain Cleaning and Ultrasonic Bath ($150)** — Professional-level deep clean of all drivetrain parts to restore operational efficiency.

Every bike is a little different, so if yours needs something in between, we will quote it before we touch a wrench. Call (703) 754-1911 or stop by the shop and we will help you pick the right level.
