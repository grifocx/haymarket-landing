Electric bikes sold in the US are grouped into classes based on how the motor works:

- **Class 1** — The motor assists only while you are pedaling, up to 20 mph. No throttle. This is the most widely accepted class on trails and paths, and it feels the most like riding a regular bike.
- **Class 2** — Adds a throttle, so you can cruise without pedaling at all, up to 20 mph. Pedal assist is still included. Handy if you want a break on hills or a boost from a stop with a loaded bike.

Both classes top out at 20 mph of motor assistance; beyond that you are pedaling on your own, just with a heavier bike. In Virginia, Class 1 and Class 2 e-bikes are generally treated like bicycles, but individual trails and parks can set their own rules, and throttle use is not welcome everywhere Class 1 is.
