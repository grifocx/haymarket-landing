Here is how drop-off and pickup work:

- **Drop-off** — Bring your bike to the shop at 4414 Costello Way in Haymarket during open hours (Tue–Sat 11 AM–6 PM, Sun 12 PM–5 PM). No appointment needed, though calling ahead at (703) 754-1911 helps us give your bike proper time.
- **Pickup** — We will call you when the work is done. The bike stays safe in the shop until you can get here.

We do not currently offer pickup or delivery per se, and we do not keep a stock of loaner bikes for repair customers. If being without a bike for a few days is a problem, or you are unable to come into the store, give us a call and we can discuss options, and we will always be upfront about the timeline before you commit.

Call (703) 754-1911 and we will work out the arrangement that gets you riding again soonest.
