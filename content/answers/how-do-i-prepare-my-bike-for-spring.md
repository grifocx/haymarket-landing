A bike that sat all winter needs about ten minutes of checking before its first spring ride. Run through this list:

1. **Tires** — Inflate to the pressure printed on the sidewall. If a tire will not hold air overnight, the tube or sealant needs attention.
2. **Brakes** — Squeeze the levers while walking the bike. The bike should stop firmly and evenly; spongy levers or a lever that reaches the handlebar means service is due.
3. **Chain** — It should be clean and lightly oiled. Rust, grit, or a squeak means it needs cleaning at minimum — and a badly worn chain wears out the rest of the drivetrain with it.
4. **Gears** — Shift through every gear. Hesitation, clunking, or a chain that jumps means an adjustment.
5. **The quick check** — Spin both wheels (rubbing or wobble), grab the handlebars and rock them (loose headset), and bounce the bike while listening for rattles.

Anything on that list fails, or the whole bike just feels stale — bring it in.
