Kids' bikes are sized by **wheel diameter**, not frame size. Common sizes and the ages they usually fit:

- **12" wheels** — roughly ages 2–4, first pedals with training wheels
- **16" wheels** — roughly ages 4–6, confident pedaling, first brakes
- **20" wheels** — roughly ages 6–9, riding on their own around the neighborhood
- **24" wheels** — roughly ages 9–12, ready for gears and longer rides
- **26" / small adult frames** — tweens and teens, often their "real" first bike

Age is only a rough guide — **height and confidence matter more**. Your child should be able to stand flat-footed over the top tube and reach the handlebars without hunching. A bike that is too big is the number-one reason kids give up on riding.
