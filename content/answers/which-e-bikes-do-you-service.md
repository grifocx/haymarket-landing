To ensure the highest quality service and safety, we service e-bikes with a standard removable seat post and a functional bicycle drivetrain from major bicycle manufacturers.

We are unable to service scooters, electric motorcycles, throttle-only "mopeds," or models with integrated foot-pegs instead of pedals.

If you are not sure whether your e-bike qualifies, give us a call at (703) 754-1911 and we will help you check before you bring it in.
