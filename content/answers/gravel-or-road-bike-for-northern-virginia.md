The answer for most riders around Haymarket is a **gravel bike**, and here is why: the moment you leave a neighborhood, the roads out here are a mix of smooth asphalt, chip-seal, and unpaved stretches. A gravel bike shrugs off all of it; a road bike makes you plan your route around every rough patch.

- **Choose a road bike** if your rides are almost entirely paved — group rides, tempo workouts, chasing speed. You give up some versatility for less weight and quicker handling.
- **Choose a gravel bike** if your rides might touch dirt, crushed stone, or the towpath — which, out here, they probably will. Wider tires (up to 45mm on something like the Time ADHX we carry), relaxed geometry, and mounting points for bags and bottles.

The good news: modern gravel bikes are not slow. On pavement, a gravel bike with smooth tires keeps up with most road rides.
